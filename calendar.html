Google Calendar

Open Google Calendar
  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer.
  2. Choose Other calendars → From URL.
  3. Paste the KICEA subscription URL above and click Add calendar.

Apple Calendar

Subscribe with Apple Calendar
  1. On iPhone/iPad, use Calendar → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar.
  2. On Mac, use Calendar → File → New Calendar Subscription.
  3. Use the KICEA subscription URL above.

Why this is a separate calendar

KICEA events are subscribed as their own calendar. Users can hide/show it without affecting their personal calendars, and can unsubscribe/remove it whenever they want. The subscription is read-only.

The subscription URL must point to a publicly reachable .ics feed. Keep the feed at the same URL so existing subscribers continue receiving future updates.
