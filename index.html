Everything you need to maintain your religious practices while in Korea

Moonsighting in Korea

Korea follows local moonsighting practices. Key points to remember:

  • •Korean Islamic organizations announce sightings
  • •Time zone: Korea Standard Time (KST, UTC+9)
  • •Consult with local Shia community for confirmation
  • •Apps: Use Islamic calendar apps set to Korea timezone
🕌

Mosques & Prayer Spaces

Finding places to pray in Korea:

  • •Seoul Central Masjid - Itaewon (main mosque)
  • •Check for Shia-specific prayer spaces in major cities
  • •University prayer rooms available at some campuses
  • •Qibla direction: Approximately west-northwest

Friday & Eid Prayers

Food & Halal Guide

Navigate Korean cuisine while maintaining halal dietary requirements

🔍

Check Ingredients

How to verify if products are halal:

  • •Use image translator apps (Papago, Google Translate)
  • •Look for these haram ingredients: 돼지 (pork), 술 (alcohol), 젤라틴 (gelatin)
  • •Common additives to watch: E-numbers, 쇼트닝 (shortening)
  • •Download halal checker apps
🍽️

Halal Restaurants

Where to eat halal in Korea:

  • •Itaewon area has many halal restaurants
  • •Look for KMF (Korea Muslim Federation) certification
  • •Use apps like HalalTrip or Zabihah
  • •Many Turkish, Pakistani, and Arab restaurants available
🛒

Halal Groceries

Where to buy halal products:

  • •Itaewon halal meat shops
  • •Online: Halal Korea, Muslim Market
  • •International markets in major cities
  • •Some E-mart and Costco locations carry halal items
🍜

Safe Korean Dishes

Korean foods that can be halal:

  • ✓Bibimbap (vegetable rice bowl - ask for no pork)
  • ✓Kimbap (Korean rice roll - verify ingredients)
  • ✓Seafood dishes (haemul pajeon, etc.)
  • ✓Tteokbokki (rice cakes - check sauce ingredients)
⚠️

Watch Out For

Common haram ingredients in Korea:

  • ✗돼지고기 (pork) - very common in Korean cuisine
  • ✗술/소주 (alcohol) - used in many marinades
  • ✗미림 (mirin) - cooking wine
  • ✗돼지 육수 (pork broth) - in many soups
🥗

Safe Alternatives

Easy halal options:

  • •Vegetarian restaurants (Buddhist temples nearby)
  • •Seafood-only restaurants
  • •International cuisine (halal-certified)
  • •Self-cooking with verified halal ingredients

Cultural Integration

Navigate Korean culture while maintaining your identity:

Explaining Dietary Restrictions

Say: "저는 종교적인 이유로 돼지고기와 술을 못 먹어요" (I can't eat pork or alcohol for religious reasons)

Work/Social Dinners

Politely decline alcohol, suggest halal restaurants, or eat beforehand and attend for socializing

Respect & Understanding

Koreans are generally respectful of religious practices. Brief explanations are usually well-received

Hijab & Modest Dress

Generally accepted. Major cities like Seoul are diverse and accommodating
